Why RadioAndroid 6.0?
Everything you need, nothing you don't
🚗

Full Android Auto

Full integration, not just audio. Control the app safely while driving — Google-reviewed and certified.

📺

Chromecast TV & ChromeOS

Works on TV boxes, smart TVs, Chromebooks and laptops over home LAN/Wi-Fi.

🎵

Background Playback

Music keeps playing while you use other apps. Foreground service ensures it never stops.

🌍

Global Streams

Thousands of radio stations worldwide. Rock, Jazz, News, Sports and more via fmstream.org.

📱

Bluetooth Ready

Works with Android Auto, classic Bluetooth, headphones and Boomboxes. BT watch control supported. A Hi-Fi amplifier with built-in Bluetooth controls the app the same way as headphones or the steering-wheel buttons in a car — so the amplifier's own remote control can do next / previous station and play / stop.

🎚️

10-Band Equalizer

Professional equalizer from 60 Hz to 14 kHz powered by LibVLC engine.

🔧

Hybrid Audio Engine

LibVLC plays ICY, MP3, AAC and Ogg streams. For HLS streams (.m3u8) RadioAndroid uses ExoPlayer. The app picks the right engine for the stream — nothing to configure.

🛡️

Stream Stability

Two-layer protection with advanced reconnect and watchdog system to keep streams alive.

💾

JSON / PLS Import & Export

Manage playlists in JSON (RadioAndroid) or PLS (VLC, etc.) format. Easy backup and sharing.

🛠️

Aftermarket Head Units & Tablets

Runs on aftermarket Android car head units and tablets that are not Google-certified — the kind sold as 2-DIN replacements. Installs straight from the Play Store on those devices, and works in split screen next to your navigation app.

🪟

Windows Desktop Port

The same C# codebase and LibVLC engine now run on Windows 11 — equalizer, metadata, cover art, skins, sleep timer and a real VU meter that measures the sound the app plays — left and right, five bands, following the volume. Coming to the Microsoft Store, planned by the end of 2026.

📡

Wi-Fi → LTE Fallback

Automatically switches between Wi-Fi and mobile data. During the switch there is a short dropout — then the app resumes playback by itself, no tap needed.

Playlist Management
JSON & PLS Format

Both formats fully supported — save, load and edit manually in any text editor. PLS is compatible with VLC and Winamp. JSON is RadioAndroid's native format and also stores your favourites.

💾 JSON — RadioAndroid native
[{"Nazwa":"RMF FM",
"Sciezka":"http://rs201-krk.rmfstream.pl/rmf_fm"},
{"Nazwa":"BBC World",
"Sciezka":"http://stream.live.vc.bbcmedia.co.uk/bbc_world_service"}]

Stores stations + favourites · human-readable · easy to edit

📋 PLS — VLC / Winamp compatible
[playlist]
NumberOfEntries=2
File1=http://rs201-krk.rmfstream.pl/rmf_fm
Title1=RMF FM
File2=http://stream.live.vc.bbcmedia.co.uk/bbc_world_service
Title2=BBC World

Universal format · works with VLC, Winamp and RadioAndroid
