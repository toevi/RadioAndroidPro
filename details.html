Under the Hood
Why LibVLC matters for internet radio

Most Android radio apps rely on Android's built-in MediaPlayer or Google's ExoPlayer. RadioAndroid 6.0 uses LibVLC — the same engine behind VLC Media Player, trusted by 300+ million users worldwide. For internet streams, the difference is significant. For HLS streams (.m3u8) the app switches to ExoPlayer; the engine is chosen automatically per stream.

🌐

Protocol Coverage

Handles HTTP, HTTPS, AAC, MP3, Ogg Vorbis, DASH, ICY metadata and more — natively, without third-party plugins. HLS (.m3u8) streams are played by ExoPlayer.

LibVLC for ICY · ExoPlayer for HLS
🔁

Stream Reconnection

VLC's internal buffer management handles stream drops at the engine level. RadioAndroid adds a second app-level watchdog on top for double protection.

2-layer stability system
🎚️

Native 10-Band Equalizer

The EQ operates directly in the VLC audio pipeline — not via Android's AudioEffect API. This means it works on all streams, including low-bitrate and AAC.

60 Hz → 14 kHz · 10 bands
⚡

Low Latency Buffering

VLC's intelligent pre-buffering reduces stream startup time and minimises gaps when switching stations — especially on slow mobile connections.

Adaptive buffer management
📡

Cellular Fallback

When Wi-Fi drops, the app transparently switches to LTE/5G without interrupting playback. Handled at VLC transport layer.

Wi-Fi → LTE seamless switch
🔑

Private & Authenticated Streams

Services like RadioTunes, di.fm and similar platforms protect their premium streams with a personal access key embedded in the URL. RadioAndroid handles these natively — just paste your full personal stream URL and it plays instantly, exactly like any public station. Because LibVLC treats authenticated HTTP streams the same as open ones, it is likely to work with other subscription services that use the same approach.

RadioTunes · di.fm · personal key URLs · and more
🔓

Open Source · LGPL

LibVLC is open-source under LGPL licence. No vendor lock-in, no telemetry, no black-box audio processing.

Trusted by 300M+ users globally
10-Band LibVLC Equalizer — native audio pipeline

↑ Click any band to randomise the preset. The EQ runs inside the VLC audio pipeline — not Android's AudioEffect API — so it works on every stream format, including low-bitrate AAC and Ogg Vorbis. Presets can be saved and loaded by name at any time.
